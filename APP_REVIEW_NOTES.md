## Notes for the reviewer (paste as is)

Nexdo is a task manager that tells you what to do next. Thank you for reviewing it.

**Why an account is required.** A user's tasks are saved to their account and kept in sync across their phones (iPhone and Android, signed in to the same account). A Nexdo Pro subscription also belongs to the account, so it follows the user to a new phone. Without an account, nothing could be saved or synced. You can still see the core feature before signing up: onboarding lets you speak or type your tasks and shows the plan Nexdo makes from them.

**Signing in.** Use the account above (email, then password), or "Continue with Apple" / "Continue with Google". To try sign-up instead, use any email address you can read: a 6-digit code is emailed.

**Subscriptions.** Nexdo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly; the yearly plan has a 3-day free trial. The plans screen appears near the end of onboarding, before an account exists. A purchase made there moves to the account at sign-up, and "Restore purchases" is available on every plans screen and in Settings. Everything works on the free plan. Adding tasks by hand, the Today page, focus sessions and reminders are unlimited; Pro adds Magic mic (the microphone in the tab bar) and larger monthly AI allowances. Free accounts see a padlock on Magic mic, and tapping it opens the plans. Manage or cancel: Settings → Manage subscription.

**AI features and consent.** AI features use Google's Gemini API. Before anything is sent to it, the app asks once for permission ("Use AI with Nexdo?"). Saying "Not now" keeps the app working without AI, and the choice can be changed in Settings → AI. Anything the AI writes (advice, task breakdowns, task updates) has a "Report" link under it.

**Microphone, camera, photos.** The microphone is used for Magic mic and for the onboarding voice demo. The camera and photos are used to show Nexdo a photo for a task, set a profile picture, or attach a screenshot to feedback. Each is asked only when that feature is first used.

**Account deletion.** Settings → tap your name at the top → Delete account. It deletes the account and all its data from our servers right away. There's also a web page: https://getnexdo.app/delete-account/
