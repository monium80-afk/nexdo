## Notes for the reviewer (paste as is)

Nexdo is a task manager that tells you what to do next. Thank you for reviewing it.

**Why an account is required.** A user's tasks are saved to their account and kept in sync across their phones (iPhone and Android, signed in to the same account). A Nexdo Pro subscription also belongs to the account, so it follows the user to a new phone. Without an account, nothing could be saved or synced. You can still see the core feature before signing up: during onboarding you can say everything on your mind for up to a minute (or type it), and Nexdo turns it into a sorted list of tasks.

**Signing in.** On the first screen, tap "I already have an account", then "Log in with email": enter the email above, tap Continue, then the password. "Log in with Google" and "Log in with Apple" also work. To try sign-up instead, go through onboarding and use any email address you can read: a 6-digit code is emailed. If you said tasks during onboarding and then log in to an existing account, the app asks whether to add them.

**Subscriptions.** Nexdo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly; the yearly plan has a 3-day free trial. The plans screen appears near the end of onboarding, before an account exists; it opens on Pro, and a Free / Pro switch at the top shows what each plan includes. A purchase made there moves to the account at sign-up, and "Restore" is available on every plans screen and in Settings. The free plan is the app without AI, and everything in it works: adding tasks by hand (the + at the top of the Today and Tasks pages), the Today page, the Calendar, focus sessions and reminders are unlimited. Pro adds every AI feature, each with a monthly allowance: Magic mic (the microphone in the middle of the tab bar), "Break it down" in a focus session (it can first read a photo, a file or a spoken description of the task), voice notes, and notes, photos and documents the AI reads for a task. On a free account each of these shows a padlock, and tapping it opens the plans. Manage or cancel: Settings → Manage.

**AI features and consent.** AI features use Google's Gemini API. Before anything is sent to it, the app asks once for permission ("Use AI with Nexdo?"). Saying "Not now" keeps the app working without AI, and the app asks again the next time an AI feature is used. During onboarding, before sign-in, the brain-dump demo can stream up to one minute of audio directly from the phone to Google so tasks can appear while the user speaks; the audio isn't recorded or kept. Anything the AI writes (task breakdowns, task updates and their advice) has a "Report" link under it.

**Microphone, camera, photos.** The microphone is used for Magic mic, the onboarding brain dump (up to one minute, before sign-up, after the AI permission question; the audio isn't kept), and to describe a task out loud before "Break it down". The camera and photos are used to show Nexdo a photo for a task, set a profile picture, or attach a screenshot to feedback. Each is asked only when that feature is first used.

**Account deletion.** Settings → tap your name at the top → Delete account. It deletes the account and all its data from our servers right away. There's also a web page: https://getnexdo.app/delete-account/
